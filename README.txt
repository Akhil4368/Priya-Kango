BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE — QUICK SETUP

1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep the folder structure as-is:
   birthday-surprise/
     index.html
     css/style.css
     js/script.js
     photos/
     videos/
     audio/

3. Add your files:
   - photos/photo1.jpg through photos/photo4.jpg
   - videos/memory1.mp4
   - videos/birthday.mp4
   - videos/final-surprise.mp4 (optional)
   - audio/music.mp3

4. Open index.html in a browser to test locally.
   Note: some browsers restrict audio/video when opened as a local file.
   For the QR code to work for your girlfriend, publish the folder online
   (GitHub Pages, Netlify, or another HTTPS static host) and make the QR
   code point to the published website URL.

CUSTOMIZATION:
- Change her name and your letter in index.html.
- Change captions under photos in index.html.
- Edit the 23 reasons in js/script.js.
- Change colors and design in css/style.css.

IMPORTANT:
The QR code must point to the hosted HTTPS website URL, not to index.html
on your computer. Do not rename the asset files unless you also update
their paths in index.html.

NEW PREMIUM FEATURES:
- Floating quick navigation bar for phone users
- Reading progress bar at the top
- Tap any gallery photo to open a full-screen lightbox; use arrows or Escape to navigate/close
- Four "Open When You Need a Smile" personal note cards
- Additional mobile responsive styling


NEW ROMANTIC FEATURES
- Your Secret Love Box: tap the gift box on the page to reveal a personal love note.
- Voice message: put your own MP3 voice recording at audio/voice.mp3.
- Final virtual hug: after opening the final gift, tap “Tap for one last hug”.

For your voice recording, record a message on your phone, save/export it as MP3, name it voice.mp3, and place it in the audio folder. If you do not add it, the voice player will have no audio to play.
