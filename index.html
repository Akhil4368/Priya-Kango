Sunno, mujhe pata hai ki shayad hum hamesha ke liye ek dusre ke saath rehne ke liye nahi bane hain. Kabhi-kabhi mujhe feel hota hai ki shayad tum ab humare relationship ko khatam karna chahti ho. 🥺

Lekin mera dil bas itna chahta hai ki jab tak hum saath hain, tab tak tumhare saath kuch khoobsurat pal jee loon. Tumhare saath thoda aur has loon, tumhari baatein sun loon, tumhe dekh loon aur aisi memories bana loon jinhe baad mein yaad karke mere face par smile aa jaaye. ❤️

Tumhare saath bitaya hua har pal mujhe kam lagta hai. Ghanton baat kar loon, phir bhi lagta hai kaash thodi der aur baat ho jaati. Tumhare saath poora din bita loon, phir bhi lagta hai din kitni jaldi khatam ho gaya.

Mujhe nahi pata humara future kya hai, lekin jab tum mere saath hoti ho na, toh sab kuch thoda achha lagne lagta hai. Main bas apne aaj mein tumhare saath thoda sa sukoon dhoondhna chahta hoon.

Kabhi-kabhi darr lagta hai ki ek din yeh sab sirf memories bankar reh jaayega—tumhari awaaz, tumhari hasi, tumhari baatein aur woh chhoti-chhoti moments. Isiliye main tumhare saath bitaye har pal ko thoda aur jeena chahta hoon.

Main nahi chahta ki tum sirf meri khushi ke liye mere saath raho. Tumhari feelings aur tumhari happiness bhi mere liye important hain. Main tumhe force nahi karna chahta. Bas agar tumhare dil mein abhi bhi humare liye thodi si jagah hai, toh main chahta hoon ki hum saath mein bache hue waqt ko achhe se jeeyein. 🫶

Mujhe tumse poori zindagi ka promise nahi chahiye, mujhe bas tumhare saath bitaye hue in palon mein thodi si aur zindagi chahiye. ❤️

Agar ek din humein alag hona bhi pada, toh main chahta hoon ki humare paas ek dusre ke liye sirf complaints nahi, balki khoobsurat memories bhi hon. Aisi memories jinhe yaad karke hum keh sakein ki humne ek waqt par ek dusre ko dil se khush kiya tha.

Mujhe nahi pata kismat ne humare liye kya likha hai. Bas aaj jab tum mere saath ho, main is aaj ko dil se jeena chahta hoon.

Tumhare saath kitna bhi waqt bita loon, dil hamesha kehta hai—kaash thoda sa aur waqt tumhare saath mil jaaye. 🥹❤️
